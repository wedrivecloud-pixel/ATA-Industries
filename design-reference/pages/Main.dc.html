01 / Logo direction

A bent-sheet “A” with a laser-cut crossbar.

The mark reads as a formed profile seen end-on, with the crossbar as a single cut line in arc orange. The wordmark sets ATA in wide Archivo ExtraBold over a tracked mono descriptor. Direction only: have it redrawn as vector artwork before launch.
