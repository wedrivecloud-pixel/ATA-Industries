Our history

Started to make life easier for local farmers.

  1. 1960

    Founded as a small job shop

    Lloyd Luedtke invents a hay mover and hay conditioner, then a bale thrower that was patented and manufactured for Ford.

  2. 1969

    The Elimnator

    The shop builds what it describes as the first mechanical tractor puller.

  3. 1985

    Lance Industries

    The first tumbler in the U.S., built for the food processing industry.

  4. 2018

    New ownership

    Ryan Konicek buys the company and expands the building's infrastructure and capabilities.

  5. 2025

    ISO 9001:2015 certified

    A documented quality management system, audited by a third party.

  6. 2026

    LMI Manufacturing becomes ATA Industries

    A new name for the next chapter, with the same people and the same commitment to the work.
